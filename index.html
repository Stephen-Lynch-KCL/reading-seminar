London Geometric Analysis Reading Seminar

A meeting place for researchers and students from universities across London to study geometric analysis together.

We read recent developments alongside classic papers that have shaped the field, taking time to understand the ideas as a group.

When Wednesdays 16:15–17:45
Where KCL, Strand Campus Room KIN 616
Who Across London All are welcome

About the seminar

The seminar is centred on shared reading and discussion. Speakers guide the group through a paper or circle of ideas, with questions and collective problem-solving encouraged throughout.
